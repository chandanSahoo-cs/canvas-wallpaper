# Privacy Policy for PaperTab (PaperTab)

**Last updated:** September 24, 2026

PaperTab (PaperTab) is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This browser extension is designed from the ground up to be private, local-first, and secure.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Storage

- **Zero Personal Data Collected:** PaperTab does **not** collect, harvest, track, or sell any personal information, browsing history, user accounts, or telemetry.
- **Local Client Storage Only:** All your drawings, sketches, scenes, background preferences, widget layout coordinates, and quick links are stored strictly on your local device using the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API (and fallback `localStorage`). No sketch or canvas data is ever transmitted to any external server.
- **No Cookies:** The extension does not set or track HTTP cookies or advertising identifiers.

---

### 2. Network Requests

PaperTab does not communicate with any external backend servers of its own. The only external network requests made by the extension are user-directed:

1. **Search Engine Queries:** When you submit a search query through the search bar widget, your browser navigates directly to your selected search provider (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, or Brave Search) as a standard web navigation.
2. **Website Favicons:** To display website icons in the Quick Links shortcut tiles, domains are queried via Google's public favicon service (`https://www.google.com/s2/favicons`). No user identifiers, personal information, or browsing history are attached to these requests.

---

### 3. Extension Permissions

- **`storage` & `unlimitedStorage`:** Used exclusively to store your drawings, wallpaper scenes, and widget configurations locally on your machine so they persist reliably across browser sessions without file size quota limitations.

---

### 4. Future In-App Purchases & Payments

If you choose to purchase optional premium features or upgrades in the future, transactions will be processed securely through an industry-standard payment processor (such as Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, or Chrome Web Store Payments). We do not store or process your credit card or financial credentials on our servers or within the extension.

---

### 5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or PaperTab, please reach out via the official Chrome Web Store listing support tab.
