# Privacy Policy for Canvas Wallpaper

**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

Canvas Wallpaper is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed from the ground up to be private, local-first, and offline-capable.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Tracking
- **Zero Personal Data Collected:** Canvas Wallpaper does **not** collect, store, track, or sell any personal information, browsing history, user accounts, or analytics.
- **Local Storage Only:** All your drawings, sketches, scenes, background preferences, widget layout coordinates, and quick links are stored strictly on your local device using the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API.

---

### 2. Network Requests
Canvas Wallpaper does not communicate with any external backend servers of its own. The only external network requests made by the extension are user-directed:
1. **Search Engine Queries:** When you submit a search query through the search bar widget, you are navigated directly to your chosen search engine (Google, DuckDuckGo, or Bing).
2. **Website Favicons:** To display website icons in the Quick Links widget, website domains are queried via Google's public favicon service (`https://www.google.com/s2/favicons`). No personal data or user identifiers are attached to these requests.

---

### 3. Extension Permissions
- **`storage` & `unlimitedStorage`:** Used exclusively to save your drawings, scenes, and widget configurations locally so they persist across browser sessions without size limitations.

---

### 4. Changes & Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or Canvas Wallpaper, please reach out via the Chrome Web Store support page or contact the developer directly.
